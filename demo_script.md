# Live Demo Script — SignSpeak AI

**Total time: ~3 minutes**

1. **Hook (20s)** — "18 million Indians are deaf or hard of hearing, but
   fewer than 300 certified ISL interpreters exist. SignSpeak AI closes that
   gap — fully offline, on your Snapdragon PC."

2. **Turn off Wi-Fi on camera (10s)** — visibly disable the network to prove
   the demo runs with zero connectivity.

3. **Live sign-to-speech demo (60s)** — perform 4-5 signs from the trained
   vocabulary (e.g. "hello", "thank you", "water", "help") and let the app
   caption + speak each one in real time.

4. **Show the NPU doing the work (30s)** — open Task Manager / Snapdragon
   performance overlay to show NPU utilization instead of CPU/GPU spiking,
   proving on-device acceleration.

5. **Latency/privacy comparison (30s)** — one slide: "Cloud ASL apps: ~800ms
   round-trip + your camera feed leaves the device. SignSpeak AI: <150ms,
   100% on-device."

6. **Close (20s)** — "This is built ISL-first, runs where interpreters
   can't reach, and keeps every frame private. Thank you."

## Backup plan

Record a screen capture of the live demo in advance in case of Wi-Fi/hardware
issues during presentation — play the video if live demo fails, then explain
the architecture verbally.
